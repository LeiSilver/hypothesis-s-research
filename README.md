## Research checkpoint · 26 November 2026

Theorem 9.2 shows that, for any fixed $C\ge 1$, decay of the norm with the sharp product cutoff $xy\le CN^2$ is equivalent to uniform decay of signed rectangle counting discrepancies. The equivalence is unconditional for the physical arithmetic form. Under modulus-specific GRH, smallness of the full Hardy-space operator implies smallness of the sharp restriction.

A proof of Hypothesis S still needs uniform cancellation in the counts near $xy\asymp N^2$ and control of the full operator across the larger retained product range.

The reproducibility section lists the commands for the finite certificates. The scripts and rational witness data are at the repository root. The manuscript acknowledges assistance from OpenAI Codex.
